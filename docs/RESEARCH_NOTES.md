# Selected Research Notes

This section publishes a small, redacted slice of ongoing empirical work around DBL-shaped systems.

The purpose is not to publish private research repositories or raw case material.
The purpose is to make one thing visible:

empirical validation is happening.

The notes here are distilled from ongoing downstream research work and rewritten for public use.

## Reading rule

These notes are:

- observational
- redacted
- non-authoritative
- not policy by themselves

They show what is being measured, compared, and stress-tested before anything is promoted into governance.

## Why this is here

DBL claims are architectural.
Architecture gets stronger when it can point to measured cases instead of only theory.

These notes exist to show:

- active empirical work
- observer-first discipline
- replay-oriented thinking
- refusal to collapse research into runtime authority too early

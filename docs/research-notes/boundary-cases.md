# Boundary Cases

Status: `observation`

This note class collects cases that are alive enough to matter and weak enough to be misleading.

The recurring pattern is not “healthy continuation” and not “resolved failure”.
It is the unstable middle:

```text
alive but fragile
```

Why this matters:

- these cases are where naive systems over-claim confidence
- they are often the first place where governance and observation visibly disagree
- they create good anchors for testing whether a boundary should stay observer-only

Working public read:

- boundary cases are better treated as comparison anchors than as immediate policy material
- they should be collected, fingerprinted, and compared against later survival/failure outcomes
- they are evidence that explicit disagreement between structure and permission is informative, not a bug
